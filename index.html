Dear Shahin,

It started in our first year. A simple request, a message asking for notes. I didn't know then that those few words would be the prologue to our story.


Then came the golden hours. We became friends, and soon, a group. The classes we bunked, the escapes to the cafe, the movies where we lost track of time. Remember the laughs in that yellow saree? For a while, the world outside our circle didn't matter.


There were moments suspended in time, quiet thoughts that lived only in the spaces between our words. Some paths are just parallel lines—meant to run alongside each other beautifully, but never quite meant to cross.


Everything was perfect for that first year, a beautiful chaos. But then a mistake was made, a fracture in the foundation. We drifted apart, walking our own ways down different paths.


There are ways to forget sometimes, but the story was beautiful while it lasted. Like a book titled 'Can we become strangers again', you are a chapter I'll always remember.

With a smile...
